This is my c34 Doomenstein Gold

Intro: You Play as a Demon, kill the marines and turn them into meat. If you share your meat with your friends, they will join you as your ally. Build your demon army and destroy the filthy human.

Here are the list of features implemented:
Player play as Demon.
2 New-ish weapon: Melee and Meat
Weapons have different functionality based on LMB/RMB pressed
While in melee, LMB to attack, RMB to block(-15 flat damage taken, reduce movement speed)
While equipped with meat, Hold and release LMB to throw(like in Pokémon Go) to tame Demons, RMB to heal.
Meat have limited ammo, can not be used when run out.
Marine drop meat on death, picking up meat restore player's ammo.
Third person camera
Demon in party will follow player in single file line, and sprint when player is sprinting.
Sprinting cause enemy to detect player more easily.
When any demon in party is aggroed onto an enemy, all demons in party while be aggroed.
Attract screen and game over screen images.
Bigger Custom map.
Balance changes on Demon and Marine actors. 
Overall AI rebalance, making them less aggressive.

Control:
WASD: movement
LMB: Use Weapon  Melee: attack, Meat: aim and throw meat to recruit ally.
RMB: Alternate use weapon Melee: hold to block, Meat: eat meat to heal

Q/E: Zoom in/ out camera

1/2: Select Weapons

Right/Left arrow: Select priev/ next weapon

~:Dev console

Esc: quit

G: press in attract to enter Gold
