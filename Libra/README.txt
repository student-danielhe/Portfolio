This is my c34 SD Libra.

Controls:
WASD: Move


Debug Control:
Xbox left stick down/T : Toggle Slow mode
Xbox Dpad UP/P: Pause
Xbox Dpad Down/O: Advance 1 frame
Xbox back/Q: Quit
Xbox right stick down /F1: Toggle Debug view
Xbox Dpad Left/F8: Force Restart
